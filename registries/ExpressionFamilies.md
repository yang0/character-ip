# Expression Families Registry

> 本文件是人格到视觉表情的轻量映射库。不是固定人格绑定；由 Personality Engine 按当前用户动态匹配。

## E01 — Deadpan
- face: low-energy neutral mouth, relaxed eyelids
- brow: straight / minimal movement
- gaze: direct or slightly off-center
- vibe: “我听到了，但我不想评价”
- affinity: deadpan, dry, calm, understated

## E02 — Skeptical
- face: slight eye narrowing, flat mouth
- brow: one brow subtly raised or brows lightly drawn together
- gaze: evaluating
- head: slight tilt
- affinity: skeptical, analytical, sharp, observant

## E03 — Socially Tired
- face: lowered eyelids, neutral or slightly compressed mouth
- posture: relaxed shoulders, low tension
- affinity: tired, burnt-out, competent, dry

## E04 — Faintly Smug / Knowing
- face: asymmetric tiny mouth-corner lift, calm eyes
- brow: subtly confident
- affinity: clever, self-assured, slightly smug, witty

## E05 — Awkward / Shy
- face: restrained mouth, slight visual hesitation
- gaze: slightly averted
- posture: mildly compact
- affinity: awkward, introverted, cautious, gentle

## E06 — Playful / Cheeky
- face: lively eyes, small mischievous mouth cue
- head: slight dynamic tilt
- affinity: playful, mischievous, clever, expressive

## E07 — Focused / Analytical
- face: concentrated eye direction, neutral mouth
- posture: stable and composed
- affinity: analytical, precise, logical, competent

## E08 — Restrained Serious
- face: calm closed mouth, firm eye contact
- brow: controlled
- affinity: serious, disciplined, composed, dependable

## E09 — Blank / Confused
- face: slightly widened or simplified blank eyes, tiny neutral mouth
- head: slight tilt
- affinity: confused, weird, self-aware, comedic

## E10 — Quietly Amused
- face: restrained eye-smile or barely visible mouth lift
- affinity: warm, witty, calm, quietly funny

## E11 — Unimpressed
- face: half-lidded eyes, horizontal mouth
- affinity: unimpressed, cynical, sharp, dry

## E12 — Curious / Observant
- face: attentive eyes, relaxed mouth
- head: slight forward/side orientation
- affinity: curious, observant, intelligent, open

## E13 — Soft / Warm
- face: relaxed eyes, gentle neutral-to-soft mouth
- no broad smile required
- affinity: gentle, warm, thoughtful, approachable

## E14 — Cool / Rebellious
- face: confident low-tension eyes, neutral or slight asymmetric mouth
- posture: asymmetric lean
- affinity: rebellious, cool, independent, fearless

## E15 — Alert / Slightly Anxious
- face: attentive eyes, subtly tense mouth
- posture: modest shoulder tension
- affinity: anxious, cautious, alert, self-aware

## E16 — Weird / Chaotic-Calm
- face: unusual but controlled asymmetry, calm delivery
- affinity: weird, chaotic, clever, internet-native

---

# Global Diversity Rules

- 每轮 25 格至少覆盖 9 个 Expression Families。
- 任一 Expression Family 最多 5 格。
- 明显微笑 / 甜笑 / 礼貌笑合计最多 5 格。
- 不得因真人参考图在笑而把 smile 作为默认表达。
- 人格必须通过 expression + gaze + head pose + body pose 被看见。
